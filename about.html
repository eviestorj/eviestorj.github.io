My story

I'm finishing my journalism degree at UNL this December with minors in theatre arts and English. That mix isn't an accident. The stage taught me how to read a room, and journalism taught me how to tell people what's happening in it.

I started out covering culture for the Daily Nebraskan, interviewing actors and reporting on Lied Center performances and the weekend events that make Lincoln feel like Lincoln. From there I moved to the Flatwater Free Press in Omaha, where I helped bring in-depth Nebraska journalism to a bigger audience on social media.

Whether I'm copyediting in AP style, planning a content calendar or interviewing someone before curtain, I'm after the same thing: making people care about the story in front of them.
