Nice to meet you!

My name is Evie Storjohann. I was born and raised in Blair, Nebraska, where I learned about broadcast and print journalism at Blair High School. I also hit the stage in seven mainstage productions, where my love for musical theatre grew immensely. I moved to Lincoln to attend the University of Nebraska in 2022 in pursuit of a journalism degree, with minors in theatre arts and English. I can't wait to graduate in December 2026.

Behind the curtain

I learned the foundations of journalism, content creation and copyediting at the Daily Nebraskan. As a culture reporter, I wrote on the beats of the Lied Center and weekend and nightlife events, telling the stories behind the curtain and getting to know the "why" behind every production. As a social copyeditor, I made stories connect with a student audience.

I spent several semesters at Nebraska Nightly, the student broadcasting program in the journalism college, where I wrote clear and concise scripts, anchored productions and reported on the Iowa caucuses in the 2024 election cycle.

Making stories travel

In the summer and fall of 2025 I was the primary social media editor for the Flatwater Free Press, a role I was honored to take. I scheduled daily posts that carried the powerful storytelling of Flatwater reporters to new audiences and planned the campaign for the annual Flatwater Festival.
