📰 Why this game matters

This game is made possible for the COJMC JOUR 304 Multimedia course.

Family cattle ranches are disappearing in Nebraska. According to the Flatwater Free Press, America has lost 4 in 10 cattle ranches since 1978. In Nebraska, the number of ranches went from 17,707 beef cow operations in 2017 to 14,834 in 2022, according to the USDA Census numbers. In Nebraska, drought has sped up that decline. The Nebraska Farm Bureau reported that 86% of Nebraska’s cattle were in drought areas. Cornhusker Cattle Tycoon puts you in the boots of a Nebraska rancher facing the common issues and pressures, from rising costs to drought.

Sources: Flatwater Free Press · UNL BeefWatch · Nebraska Farm Bureau
